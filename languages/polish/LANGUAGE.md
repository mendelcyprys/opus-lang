## Script & typing
- Latin + diacritics **ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż** — always write them correctly.
- Learner drops diacritics/letters when typing on the phone (Bedę, zadwoniłeś) → correct every time; tip: long-press on the Polish keyboard.
  Diacritics can change meaning: *zjedz* (eat up) vs *zjedź* (drive down).

## Grammar map (A1 → B2)
- **A1–A2:** gender, present-tense conjugation groups, cases in the singular (acc/gen/loc/instr/dat), past tense, personal pronouns, numerals basics, adjective agreement, comparatives.
- **B1:** aspect (pairs, perfective future, time clauses, imperatives), verbs of motion + prefixes, plural cases (gen/instr/loc), numerals + case, conditional (gdyby, żeby), reported speech.
- **B2:** participles (-ący, -ny/-ty), verbal nouns (-anie/-enie), adverbial participles (-ąc, -wszy), passive (być/zostać + participle), impersonal -no/-to, collective numerals (dwoje, troje), word formation.

## Pitfalls
- Aspect choice (see `progress/mistakes.md`), consonant alternations (w Polsce), genitive of negation, *w → we* before clusters (we Włoszech).
- Pronunciation: sz/ś/cz/ć/rz/ż, nasal vowels ą/ę; stress on the penultimate syllable.
- **Interference with Ukrainian** (learner's other Slavic language): false friends *owoce* (fruit) vs *овочі* (vegetables),
  *dywan* (carpet) vs *диван* (sofa), *sklep* (shop) vs *склеп* (crypt), *zawód* (profession) vs *завод* (factory);
  separate clitic *się* vs suffix *-ся*; formal *Pan/Pani* + 3rd person vs Ukrainian *ви*.
