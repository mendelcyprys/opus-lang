# Arabic (العربية) — teaching notes

- **Folder:** `languages/arabic` · **TTS tag:** `ar-SA` for MSA; Egyptian `ar-EG`; Levantine try `ar-JO`/`ar-LB`, fall back to `ar-SA` · **Levels:** CEFR
- **Variety:** decided at onboarding and recorded in `profile.md` — MSA (Fuṣḥā: reading, news, formal), a spoken dialect
  (Levantine / Egyptian / Gulf / Maghrebi), or MSA + one dialect. Always label dialect-only forms as such.

## Script & typing
- Right-to-left, 28 letters, connected letter forms; hamza seats (أ إ ؤ ئ ء), tāʾ marbūṭa ة, alif maqṣūra ى.
  Put each Arabic sentence on its own line (see CLAUDE.md → RTL).
- **Harakat** (fatḥa, kasra, ḍamma, sukūn, shadda, tanwīn): show on new vocabulary and in examples while the learner is below ~B1;
  unvowelled text for reading practice later.
- Learner types on the Arabic phone keyboard without harakat — accept unvowelled answers.
  If they prefer, accept **Arabizi** (3 = ع, 7 = ح, 5 = خ, 2 = ء) for typed answers — ask once and note it in `profile.md`.
- **Transliteration** (vocab column, and in chat while needed): ā ī ū; ʾ hamza, ʿ ʿayn; ḥ ح, kh خ, gh غ, sh ش, th ث, dh ذ, q ق;
  emphatics ṣ ḍ ṭ ẓ.

2. Can you read the script? With/without harakat? Need transliteration? Type in Arabic script or Arabizi?

## Grammar map (A1 → B2; MSA — adapt for a dialect)
- **A1:** alphabet & joining rules, sun/moon letters with al-, pronouns, nominal sentences (no "to be" in the present), gender & ة,
  possessive suffixes, iḍāfa basics, demonstratives, numbers 1–10, questions.
- **A2:** past (perfect) & present (imperfect) of form I, negation (lā, mā, lam, lan, laysa), sound plurals, common broken-plural patterns,
  adjective agreement (non-human plural → feminine singular), prepositions + suffixes.
- **B1:** verb forms II–X, maṣdar, active/passive participles, subjunctive (an/lan) & jussive (lam), kāna and sisters, inna and sisters,
  relative clauses (alladhī), dual, numbers + counted noun.
- **B2:** passive, weak verbs (hollow, defective, assimilated), conditionals (in / law / idhā), case endings (iʿrāb) for formal reading, media Arabic.
- **Dialect instead:** replace iʿrāb and moods with dialect present prefix (b- in Levantine/Egyptian), dialect negation (mā … -sh),
  future (raḥ / ḥa-), and dialect vocabulary.

## Pitfalls
- Emphatic vs plain consonants (س/ص, ت/ط, د/ض, ذ/ظ), ع and ح, invisible short vowels, unpredictable broken plurals,
  non-human plural agreement, numbers 3–10 with reverse gender.
- **Interference with Hebrew:** shared roots help (k-t-b "write", s-l-m "peace") but watch false friends: لحم *laḥm* (meat) vs לֶחֶם (bread).
  Arabic keeps sounds Hebrew merged (ح/خ, ع), has case endings and the dual in active use.
