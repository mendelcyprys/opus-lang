# Ukrainian (українська) — teaching notes

- **Folder:** `languages/ukrainian` · **TTS tag:** `uk-UA` · **Levels:** CEFR
- **Standard:** modern standard Ukrainian (2019 orthography). Avoid Russianisms/surzhyk; if the learner produces a Russian-like form, give the standard Ukrainian one.

## Script & typing
- Cyrillic with Ukrainian letters **і ї є ґ**, the apostrophe (м'ясо, п'ять) and ь. Ukrainian has no ы э ъ ё — correct any that leak in.
- Phone: use the **Ukrainian** keyboard layout (not Russian). Transliteration only if the learner can't read Cyrillic fluently yet (ask).
- **Stress** is free and mobile → mark it with an acute (U+0301) on every new word and in example sentences for words below mastery 3
  (доро́га, руки́ / ру́ки). Unmarked running text for reading practice at higher levels.

## Notation in files
- Like Polish: nouns with gender + genitive sg.; verbs with aspect pair (impf/pf) + 1sg/2sg present if irregular or stress shifts; example sentence.
- Vocabulary columns: Ukrainian (stress marked) | English | Grammar info | Example sentence | First learned | Last reviewed | Mastery.

## Grammar map (A1 → B2)
- **A1:** alphabet & sounds (г [ɦ] vs ґ [g], и vs і, ї, є), gender, present tense (1st/2nd conjugation), pronouns, acc./loc. basics,
  **vocative** (used daily: Олено! пане Петре!), past tense (gendered, no person endings), numbers.
- **A2:** all 7 cases sg., aspect pairs, future (буду + inf., synthetic читатиму, perfective future), motion verbs (іти/ходити, їхати/їздити),
  imperatives, мій/свій, comparatives.
- **B1:** plural cases, numerals + case, prefixed motion verbs, conditional (би), passive participles (-ний/-тий), adverbial participles
  (-учи/-ючи, -вши), reflexive -ся/-сь, impersonal -но/-то (зроблено).
- **B2:** participle & verbal-noun style, complex sentences, register (official vs colloquial), word formation.
- **Leverage Polish:** aspect, cases, -no/-to, motion verbs work almost the same — reuse the learner's Polish strengths explicitly,
  pitched about one level below their Polish.

## Pitfalls
- **Interference with Polish:** false friends *овочі* (vegetables) vs *owoce* (fruit), *диван* (sofa) vs *dywan* (carpet),
  *склеп* (crypt) vs *sklep* (shop — Ukr. магазин/крамниця), *завод* (factory) vs *zawód* (profession);
  -ся attached to the verb (not separate like *się*); formal address = *ви* + 2nd pl. (not Pan/Pani + 3rd sg.);
  past tense has no person endings (я читав vs czytałem); stress is free (Polish: always penultimate); no nasal vowels.
- Russian interference (common online): кот vs **кіт**, да vs **так**, ы/э spellings.
