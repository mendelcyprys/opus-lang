- **Folder:** `languages/hebrew` · **TTS tag:** `he-IL` · **Levels:** CEFR (learner may describe ulpan levels: Aleph ≈ A1–A2, Bet ≈ A2–B1, Gimel ≈ B1 …)
- **Standard:** Modern Israeli Hebrew unless the learner asks for Biblical/liturgical.

## Script & typing
- Right-to-left; final letter forms **ך ם ן ף ץ**. Put each Hebrew sentence on its own line (see CLAUDE.md → RTL).
- **Niqqud** (vowel points): show it on new vocabulary and in example sentences while the learner is below ~B1;
  use normal unpointed text (ktiv male, plene spelling) for reading practice from ~B1.
- Learner types on the Hebrew phone keyboard without niqqud — accept unpointed answers; correct plene spelling.
- **Transliteration** (vocab column, and in chat while needed): kh for ח/כ, ts for צ, ' for a meaningful glottal stop;
  mark stress with an acute only when it is NOT on the last syllable (yéled, but shalom).

## Onboarding questions
1. Can you read Hebrew print? With or without niqqud? Need transliteration?
2. Background: ulpan, school, Biblical/prayer Hebrew, time in Israel?
3. Goal: conversation/travel, reading news, family, texts?
4. Address you in masculine forms? (`learner.md` assumes masculine — confirm, it affects every "you".)

## Grammar map (A1 → B2)
- **A1:** alphabet, niqqud basics, pronouns, present tense (4 forms m/f sg/pl), gender & plural, definite article ה, יש/אין,
  prepositions with suffixes (לי, לך, שלי), numbers, questions.
- **A2:** past & future pa'al, את (direct-object marker), infinitives, binyanim pi'el / hif'il / hitpa'el (present & past),
  roots & patterns, smichut basics, comparatives.
- **B1:** all 7 binyanim incl. passives (nif'al, pu'al, huf'al), full smichut, relative ש, conditionals (אם / אילו), verbal nouns (mishkalim),
  number gender polarity (שלוש / שלושה), reading unpointed text.
- **B2:** formal/literary register, nominal style, news Hebrew, idioms, slang awareness.

## Pitfalls
- Gender agreement everywhere (verbs in present/past/future, adjectives, numbers — numbers 3–10 take "opposite" gender forms).
- ח and כ sound the same; ו/י double as vowels; unpointed words are ambiguous (ספר = sefer / safar / sapar / siper).
- **Interference with Arabic:** many shared roots help (כ-ת-ב / ك-ت-ب "write", ש-ל-ם / س-ل-م "peace"), but watch false friends:
  לֶחֶם *lechem* (bread) vs لحم *laḥm* (meat). Binyan ↔ form mapping is only approximate (pi'el ≈ II, hif'il ≈ IV, hitpa'el ≈ V, nif'al ≈ VII).
