## Script & typing
- **á é í ó ú ñ ü ¿ ¡** — long-press on the phone keyboard. Accents can change meaning: si/sí, tu/tú, el/él, esta/está, papa/papá.
  Correct every missing accent and missing ¿ ¡.

## Grammar map (A1 → B2)
- **A1:** ser/estar, gender & articles, present (regular + common irregulars), gustar-type verbs, hay, questions, numbers.
- **A2:** stem-changing verbs, reflexives, preterite (regular/irregular), imperfect, preterite vs imperfect, ir a + inf.,
  direct/indirect object pronouns, comparatives, estar + gerundio.
- **B1:** present subjunctive (wishes, emotion, doubt, *cuando* + subj.), imperative (affirmative/negative + pronouns), por vs para,
  perfect tenses, double object pronouns (se lo), future & conditional, relative clauses.
- **B2:** imperfect subjunctive, si-clauses (si tuviera…, si hubiera…), sequence of tenses, passive & impersonal se, reported speech,
  subjunctive in relative clauses.

## Pitfalls
- ser vs estar, preterite vs imperfect, subjunctive triggers, por vs para, gender exceptions.
- False friends with English: *embarazada* (pregnant), *actual* (current), *librería* (bookshop).
- **Interference with French:** *quitar* (remove) vs *quitter* (leave), *largo* (long) vs *large* (wide), *salir* (go out) vs *salir* (to dirty);
  Spanish drops subject pronouns (French doesn't); the ser/estar split doesn't exist in French (être).
